PROCEDURE:

--- INPUT BEGIN ---

Display Name         : PayPal Official Support

Return-Path          : bounce@unverified-mta.online
Raw Email Headers    :
Received: from mail.unverified-mta.online (192.168.1.100)
From: "PayPal Official Support" <billing@paypa1-security.com>
Reply-To: phisher-drop@attacker-host.top
Return-Path: <bounce@unverified-mta.online>
Authentication-Results: mx.google.com; spf=fail (google.com: domain of billing@paypa1-security.com does not designate 192.168.1.100 as permitted sender); dkim=fail; dmarc=fail action=quarantine
--- INPUT END ---

3. Click "Analyse Sender Authenticity".

EXPECTED OUTPUT:
- Sender Authenticity Verdict: HIGH SENDER RISK (Red Badge)
- Risk Score                 : 85+ / 100

  * SPF Status   : FAIL (Red Badge)
  * DKIM Status  : FAIL (Red Badge)
  * DMARC Status : FAIL (Red Badge)

  * "Sender domain (paypa1-security.com) does not match Reply-To domain (attacker-host.top). Replies will be routed to a different destination."

  * "Sender domain does not match Return-Path domain."
